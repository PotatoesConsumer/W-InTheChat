# Consent form: W-InTheChat study

Participant ID: ______ (written by the researcher; your name is not recorded)

**What the study is.** W-InTheChat is a KMUTT Computer Science senior project. It is a
small game where you play as a streamer and a simulated audience reacts in the chat.
We want to know how two versions of that chat feel to play with.

**What you'll do (about 25 minutes).**

1. Play the game for 5 minutes with one version of the chat, then answer 5 short
   questions.
2. Play again for 5 minutes with the other version, then answer the same questions.
3. Answer 3 final questions.

During play you can type messages to your chat, as a streamer would.

**How the chat works.** The two versions make their chat in different ways. One of
them uses a language model (an AI) that runs on this laptop; nothing you type is sent
to the internet. We won't tell you which version is which until you've finished.

Some viewers in the chat tease your play, as real stream chats do. Every line is
checked by a filter before it appears, and the chat never comments on you as a person.
If anything in the chat bothers you, tell the researcher and we'll stop.

**What we record.**

- The game events (for example "cleared a wave") and the chat lines on screen.
- **The messages you type in the chat.** Please don't type personal information.
- Your questionnaire answers.

We don't use the camera or the microphone, and we don't record your screen, face or
voice. Everything is stored on this laptop under your participant ID only. The project
report shows only totals and averages, never your messages linked to you. After the
project is graded, the logs are deleted.

**Your choice.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a
reason. Until the report is written, you can ask us to delete your data: give the
researcher your participant ID.
